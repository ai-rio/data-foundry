## Your Rights Under GDPR

### 1. Right to Information (Articles 13-14)

**What it means**: You have the right to know what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and who we share it with.

**What we provide**:
- Clear privacy notices explaining our data practices
- Information collected at the time of collection
- Details about data processing purposes
- Information about data retention periods
- Lists of categories of recipients

**Example privacy notice**:
```
Data We Collect:
- Name and contact information
- Account credentials (encrypted)
- Usage data and preferences
- Consent records
- Support communications

Why We Collect It:
- To provide our services
- To improve user experience
- For security and fraud prevention
- To comply with legal obligations

How Long We Keep It:
- Active users: As long as needed for service
- Inactive users: 365 days after last activity
- Legal requirements: As required by law

Who We Share It With:
- Service providers (under contract)
- Regulatory authorities (when required)
- Third parties with your consent
```

### 2. Right of Access (Article 15)

**What it means**: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

**What you get**:
- Complete copy of your personal data
- Information about how we're using it
- Details about who we've shared it with
- How long we plan to keep it
- Information about your rights

**How to request**:
1. Online (fastest): https://datafoundry.com/portal/requests
2. Email: privacy@datafoundry.com
3. Phone: +1-555-PRIVACY
4. Mail: Privacy Team, Data Foundry, 123 Privacy St, San Francisco, CA 94105

**What happens next**:
1. **Verification**: We verify your identity (usually within 24 hours)
2. **Collection**: We gather all your data (usually within 5 days)
3. **Delivery**: Secure download link sent to you (within 30 days total)

**Example access response**:
```
Access Report for John Doe (user_123)

Personal Data Summary:
- Profile information: Name, email, phone
- Account activity: Last login, features used
- Consent records: Active consents and history
- Communication logs: Support tickets, newsletters

Download Links:
- Personal data (JSON): [Secure Download Link]
- Consent history (PDF): [Secure Download Link]
- Processing activity (CSV): [Secure Download Link]

Links expire in 24 hours for your security.
```

### 3. Right to Rectification (Article 16)

**What it means**: You can ask us to correct inaccurate personal data.

**What we can correct**:
- Spelling errors in your name
- Incorrect contact information
- Outdated address or phone
- Wrong date of birth
- Incorrect preferences

**How to request**:
1. **Online Portal**: Edit your profile directly
2. **Email Request**: Specify exact corrections needed
3. **Phone Request**: Speak with a privacy specialist
4. **Written Request**: Clearly mark corrections needed

**Verification process**:
- For minor corrections: Simple verification
- For significant changes: Document verification required
- For identity changes: Official documents required

**Example correction**:
```
Request Type: Contact Information Update
Fields to Change:
- Email: john@oldmail.com → john@newmail.com
- Phone: 555-0101 → 555-0202
- Address: 123 Main St → 456 Oak Ave

Supporting Evidence:
- Email verification link clicked
- SMS verification completed
```

### 4. Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Article 17)

**What it means**: You can request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.

**When deletion applies**:
- You withdraw consent (where consent is the legal basis)
- Data is no longer necessary for our services
- You object to processing and there's no overriding legal reason
- Data was processed unlawfully
- Legal requirements require deletion

**What gets deleted**:
- Account profile and preferences
- Usage data and activity logs
- Consent records (except legal requirements)
- Communication history
- Marketing data

**What we must keep**:
- Audit logs (7 years for security)
- Legal requirements (as required)
- Fraud prevention records (as required)

**How deletion works**:
1. **Request**: Submit deletion request
2. **Verification**: Verify identity (24-48 hours)
3. **Review**: Check legal holds (48-72 hours)
4. **Execution**: Delete eligible data (24 hours)
5. **Confirmation**: Send deletion confirmation

**Example deletion process**:
```
Deletion Request Received: 2024-12-22 10:00
Identity Verified: 2024-12-22 11:30
Legal Hold Check: 2024-12-22 14:00
Deletion Started: 2024-12-22 15:00
Completion: 2024-12-22 16:30
```

### 5. Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)

**What it means**: You can limit how we use your data in certain situations.

**When restriction applies**:
- While verifying data accuracy
- During deletion requests
- When you object to processing
- When no longer needed but you want us to keep it

**What happens during restriction**:
- Data is marked as "restricted"
- No processing for other purposes
- Data storage continues
- Security maintained

**How to request**:
1. Specify what data to restrict
2. Explain the reason
3. Duration of restriction
4. Verification required

### 6. Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

**What it means**: You can receive your data in a machine-readable format and transfer it to another service.

**Available formats**:
- JSON (default, machine-readable)
- CSV (for spreadsheets)
- XML (structured data)
- PDF (human-readable)

**Transfer options**:
1. **Download**: Secure link for you to download
2. **Direct transfer**: Send directly to another service
3. **Physical media**: USB drive (for large datasets)
4. **API access**: For technical users

**What gets ported**:
- All personal data you provided
- Generated data about you
- Usage history and preferences
- Consent records
- Communication history

**Example portability**:
```
Data Portability Package for John Doe

Formats Available:
- JSON: 15.2 MB
- CSV: 14.8 MB (split into 3 files)
- XML: 16.1 MB
- PDF Summary: 2.3 MB

Direct Transfer Options:
- Transfer to NewService.com
- Send via encrypted email
- API access token: [30-day token]
```

### 7. Right to Object (Article 21)

**What it means**: You can object to certain types of data processing.

**Processing you can object to**:
- Direct marketing
- Profiling for marketing
- Research and statistics
- Legitimate interests processing

**What happens when you object**:
- Immediate processing stop
- Review of objection
- Response within 30 days
- Continued stop if valid

**How to object**:
1. **Online Form**: https://datafoundry.com/objections
2. **Email**: objections@datafoundry.com
3. **Phone**: +1-555-OBJECT
4. **Mail**: Privacy Team, Data Foundry

**Example objection**:
```
Objection Details:
- Processing Type: Marketing Communications
- Reason: Don't want promotional emails
- Date: 2024-12-22
- Preference: Email only for account updates

Action Taken:
- All marketing emails stopped immediately
- Retained in suppression list permanently
- Account emails continue as normal
```

### 8. Rights Related to Automated Decision Making (Article 22)

**What it means**: You have rights when decisions about you are made automatically without human intervention.

**When this applies**:
- Credit decisions
- Service eligibility
- Risk assessments
- Content recommendations

**Your rights**:
- Request human review
- Express your point of view
- Contest the decision
- Request human intervention

**How to exercise**:
1. Identify the automated decision
2. Request human review
3. Provide additional information
4. Appeal if necessary

```

[Your Name]
```

## Special Categories of Data

### Health Data

Additional protections apply to health information:
- Explicit consent required
- Separate storage and encryption
- Limited access
- Additional verification for access

### Biometric Data

Special handling for biometric information:
- Purpose limitation
- Enhanced security
- Separate consent
- Limited retention

### Children's Data

Extra protection for users under 18:
- Parental consent required under 16
- Limited data collection
- No marketing
- Special support available

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How quickly do you respond to requests?

**A**: We acknowledge all requests within 24 hours and provide a complete response within 30 days. For complex requests, we may extend to 60 days with your consent.

### Q: Is there a fee for exercising my rights?

**A**: No, exercising your rights is free. We may charge a reasonable fee for excessive or repetitive requests.

### Q: What if I'm not satisfied with your response?

**A**: You can:
1. Request an internal review
2. Escalate to our Data Protection Officer
3. File a complaint with your local supervisory authority
4. Seek legal remedy

### Q: How do you verify my identity?

**A**: We use:
- Two-factor authentication
- Document verification
- Knowledge-based authentication
- Video verification for sensitive requests

### Q: What happens to my data after deletion?

**A**: Deleted data is:
- Removed from active systems
- Deleted from backups within 90 days
- Purged from all caches
- Logged in deletion register

### Q: Can someone else make a request on my behalf?

**A**: Yes, with proper authorization:
- Signed authorization letter
- Power of attorney document
- Legal guardian documentation
- Court order
